Keep it attainable

If you’re new to working out, keep your daily routine quick so you won’t burn out early.

Don’t stress about exercising every muscle

Don’t worry about making a routine for every single muscle group right now. Just plan for a few (e.g. arms, legs, core) before moving on to new muscles.

Be consistent

The key is consistency. Just get working out a little bit each day to get the habit going, then begin adding just a little when you feel like doing more.
